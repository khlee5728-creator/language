What Is Pragmatics?

Pragmatics = the study of how context contributes to meaning
  • The same sentence can mean different things in different situations.
  • "It's cold in here" may be a statement — or a request to close the window.
  • Interpretation depends on who, where, when, and why.

※ 의미론은 문장의 문자적 의미, 화용론은 맥락 속 발화 의미를 다룬다.

화용론과 의미론의 경계를 분명히 하세요. "It's cold in here"처럼 형식은 진술문이지만 기능은 요청일 수 있다는 점이 화용론의 출발점입니다.

1) Two Kinds of Context

Linguistic Context

The surrounding discourse — earlier sentences. It lets us interpret he, it, the other one.

Situational Context

Knowledge of the world and the speech situation — who is speaking, where, when, and what they intend.

Both are needed to recover a speaker's intended meaning.

※ 언어적 맥락 = 앞뒤 담화, 상황적 맥락 = 세계 지식·발화 상황.

언어적 맥락(discourse)과 상황적 맥락(situation)을 구분하세요. 다음 슬라이드의 직시(deixis)는 상황 맥락 없이는 해석 불가능한 대표 사례입니다.

2) Deixis — Pointing with Language

Deictic expressions can only be interpreted relative to the speech situation.

PersonI · you · we · they
Placehere · there · this · that
Timenow · then · today · tomorrow

"I'll meet you here tomorrow" is meaningless without knowing who speaks, where, and when.

※ 직시 표현은 화자의 '지금-여기(here-and-now)', 즉 발화 상황·화자의 관점에 상대적으로 해석된다.

직시(deixis)의 3유형(인칭·장소·시간)을 정리하세요. 녹음된 쪽지 "Meet me here in an hour"가 왜 해석 불가한지 예로 들면 직시의 상황 의존성이 분명해집니다.

Diagram · Anchored to the Speaker

All deictic expressions are anchored to the speaker's here-and-now.

Move the speaker, and the reference of here / there / now / then shifts with them.

화자의 '지금-여기'를 축으로 인칭·장소·시간이 방사되는 그림입니다. 화자가 이동하면 지시 대상이 바뀐다는 상대성을 각인시키세요.

3) Reference & Reference Resolution

  • Reference — using an expression to pick out an entity in the world.
  • Antecedent — the earlier expression that establishes the referent.
  • Co-reference — a later pronoun co-refers with its antecedent; recovering it is reference resolution. (traditional term: anaphora)
"Sue likes pizza. She thinks it is the perfect food."

She co-refers with the antecedent Sue; it with pizza.

※ 대명사는 선행사(antecedent)와 공지시(co-refer)하며, 이 지시 대상 찾기를 지시 해소(reference resolution)라 한다.

선행사-대용어 관계는 언어적 맥락으로 지시를 계산하는 사례입니다. 대명사가 앞 명사구에 의존한다는 점을 화살표로 짚어 주세요.

4) Presupposition

Presupposition = information the speaker assumes as background
  • "Have you stopped smoking?" presupposes you smoked before.
  • "The present King of France is bald" presupposes there is a present king of France.
  • Presuppositions survive negation: "The present King of France is not bald" still assumes such a king exists.
  • Since France has no king, the utterance is simply inappropriate — presupposition failure.

※ 전제는 문장을 부정해도 유지된다 — 이것이 전제와 함의를 구별하는 진단법.

전제(presupposition)의 핵심 성질은 부정 하에서도 유지된다는 점(부정 시험, negation test)입니다. 이를 함의와 대비해 설명하세요.

5) Implicature — Grice's Cooperative Principle

The Cooperative Principle

Speakers generally try to communicate sincerely and cooperatively. The four maxims below are collectively known as Grice's cooperative principle.

A: "Is Jane a good worker?" — B: "She's usually on time."
B implicates that Jane is not especially good, without saying so.

※ 함축(implicature)은 문자적으로 말하지 않은 것을 협력 원리를 바탕으로 추론한 의미.

Grice의 협력 원리를 소개하세요. 대화 함축(conversational implicature)은 문자적 의미가 아니라 "왜 그렇게 말했는가"를 추론해 얻는 의미임을 예로 보여주세요.

The Four Maxims

Quantity
Be as informative as required — no more, no less.
Quality
Say only what you believe true and can support.
Relation
Be relevant to the current exchange.
Manner
Be clear, brief, orderly; avoid obscurity.

To violate a maxim is to be quietly uncooperative; to flout it is to break it openly, so the hearer infers a conversational implicature.

※ 격률을 몰래 어기면 위반(violate), 대놓고 어겨 함축을 유도하면 위배(flout) — 함축은 flout에서 발생한다.

네 격률(양·질·관계·태도)을 암기하되, 핵심은 격률을 '고의로 어길 때(flouting)' 함축이 발생한다는 점입니다. 반어·과장 등이 이에 해당합니다.

6) Speech Acts — Performatives

To say something is often to do something — we perform acts by speaking.

  • Performative verbs — the utterance performs the act: "I promise…", "I bet…", "I now pronounce you…".
  • The "I hereby ___" test — a verb is performative if "I hereby" sounds right: I hereby apologize ✓ vs. *I hereby know you ✗.
  • Illocutionary force — the speaker's intended act (a promise, a bet, an order), which can be conveyed even with no performative verb.

"I've got five bucks that says you're wrong" carries the illocutionary force of a bet in the right context.

※ 수행문은 'I hereby ___' 삽입이 자연스러운 문장 — 발화가 곧 행위. 화자의 의도된 행위력을 발화수반력(illocutionary force)이라 한다.

교재가 강조하는 핵심은 수행동사(performative verb)와 'I hereby' 검사, 그리고 발화수반력(illocutionary force)입니다. 수행동사가 없어도(예: 내기) 문맥상 행위력이 전달될 수 있음을 강조하세요.
Enrichment · Beyond the Textbook

Direct vs. Indirect Acts

  • Direct speech act — form matches function: "Close the door" (imperative = command).
  • Indirect speech act — form ≠ function: "Can you close the door?" (question form = request).
  • Felicity conditions — background conditions an act needs to succeed (e.g. only an authorized official can pronounce a marriage).

These Austin/Searle terms extend the chapter; the textbook itself treats "Can you pass the salt?" under implicature and politeness.

※ 직접/간접 화행과 적정 조건은 교재 밖 보충 개념(Austin·Searle). 교재는 "Can you pass the salt?"를 함축·공손성으로 다룬다.

직접/간접 화행과 적정 조건은 표준 화행론 용어지만 이 교재(Ch.4)에는 명시되지 않은 보충 내용입니다. 시험 범위와 구분해 안내하세요.

4Which maxim is most clearly flouted by an obviously exaggerated statement like "I've told you a million times"? (cf. textbook Ch.4, pp.165–167)
Explanation The speaker asserts something literally false (they have not said it a million times), flouting the maxim of Quality, which prompts the hearer to infer the intended meaning "very often / I'm frustrated."

Exercises · Based on Textbook Ch.4
5What does "Sam regrets selling his car" presuppose, even when negated? (cf. textbook Ch.4, pp.167–168)
Explanation The factive verb regret presupposes the truth of its complement. Even "Sam does not regret selling his car" still presupposes that Sam sold the car — the presupposition survives negation.

Summary

Key Points

  • Pragmatics = meaning in context (linguistic + situational)
  • Deixis — person, place, time — anchored to the speaker
  • Reference resolution: a pronoun co-refers with its antecedent; presupposition survives negation
  • Implicature arises from the Cooperative Principle & four Maxims
  • Violating vs flouting a maxim; flouting triggers conversational implicature
  • Speech acts: performative verbs, the "I hereby" test, illocutionary force
