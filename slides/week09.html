What Do Speakers Know About Meaning?

Semantics = the study of the linguistic meaning of words and sentences
  • Speakers know when two sentences mean the same (paraphrase).
  • They know when a sentence is true given another (entailment).
  • They detect contradiction and ambiguity instantly.

※ 우리는 배운 적 없이도 두 문장의 동의·함의·모순·중의성을 직관적으로 판단한다.

의미 지식도 음운·통사 지식처럼 무의식적입니다. 학생들에게 두 문장을 주고 "같은 뜻인가?"를 즉답하게 하면 이 직관을 실감시킬 수 있습니다.

1) Truth-Based Relations

Much of sentence meaning is captured by truth conditions — the situations in which a sentence is true.

  • Entailment — if A is true, B must be true. Jack ate meat ⊨ Jack ate food.
  • Paraphrase — A and B have the same truth conditions. The cat chased the dog = The dog was chased by the cat.
  • Contradiction — A cannot be true. *My unmarried sister is married.

※ 함의는 한 방향(entailment), 동의(paraphrase)는 서로 함의하는 양방향 관계.

함의(entailment)는 단방향, 동의(paraphrase)는 상호 함의로 정의됨을 강조하세요. 모순은 어떤 상황에서도 참이 될 수 없는 문장입니다.

Always True, Always False

Analytic (tautology)

True in every situation by meaning alone. Circles are round; A bachelor is unmarried.

Contradiction

False in every situation. Circles are square; *A bachelor is married.

Truth-conditional Semantic Rules (I & II) combine word meanings step by step — e.g. deriving the meaning of Jack swims from Jack and swims — to compute when a sentence is true.

※ 분석적(항진)문은 항상 참, 모순문은 항상 거짓 — 교재의 진리조건 규칙(Semantic Rule I·II)이 단어 의미를 합성해 진리값을 계산한다.

항진문(tautology, analytic)과 모순문(contradiction)을 진리조건으로 대비하세요. 교재의 Semantic Rule I·II가 단어 의미를 합성해 문장의 진리값을 도출한다는 점을 개념 수준에서 소개합니다.

Ambiguity — One Form, Two Meanings

Lexical ambiguity

A single word has two senses. She went to the bank — riverside or financial institution?

Structural ambiguity

The boy saw the man with the telescope — who has the telescope? Two structures, two meanings.

Structural ambiguity shows that meaning depends on syntactic structure, not just word choice.

※ 어휘적 중의성 = 단어의 다의, 구조적 중의성 = 통사 구조 차이에서 발생.

구조적 중의성은 다음 슬라이드의 합성성으로 자연스럽게 이어집니다. 같은 단어들이라도 구조가 다르면 의미가 달라진다는 점을 예고하세요.

2) Compositional Semantics

The Principle of Compositionality

The meaning of a sentence is built from the meanings of its words and the way they are syntactically combined.

  • Same words, different structure → different meaning.
  • The dog bit the man ≠ The man bit the dog.
  • This is why we can understand sentences we have never heard.

※ 합성성 원리 = 부분(단어)의 의미 + 결합 방식(구조) → 전체(문장)의 의미.

합성성은 문장 의미의 무한 생산을 설명하는 핵심 원리입니다. 어순·구조가 의미에 기여한다는 점을 dog/man 예로 각인시키세요.

Diagram · Building Meaning from Structure

The verb assigns thematic (theta) roles to its arguments — meaning flows from structure.

Maria handed the key to Sam. — the verb hand requires an agent, a theme, and a goal.

술어(verb)가 논항에 의미역(theta role)을 부여하는 구조입니다. 논항구조(argument structure)와 의미역이 문장 의미를 조직한다는 점을 도식으로 각인시키세요.

3) When Compositionality Fails

Idioms
Meaning is not the sum of parts. kick the bucket = die, not "strike a pail."
Anomaly
Grammatical but meaningless: Colorless green ideas sleep furiously.
Metaphor
Non-literal transfer: Time is money; the classroom was a zoo.
Why it matters
These show meaning is not always fully predictable from structure alone.

※ 관용구·의미 이상·은유는 합성성이 깨지는 대표 사례 — 부분의 합으로 전체 의미를 예측 못 함.

Chomsky의 "Colorless green ideas…"는 통사적 적형성과 의미적 적형성이 별개임을 보여주는 고전 예입니다. 관용구는 통째로 어휘부에 저장된다는 점도 언급하세요.

4) Lexical Semantics — Sense vs. Reference

Reference

The real-world entity a word points to. Superman and Clark Kent pick out the same person.

Sense

The mental concept / mode of presentation. The two names have the same reference but a different sense.

Some expressions have sense but no reference — the present King of France, a unicorn.

※ 지시(reference) = 가리키는 대상, 의미(sense) = 개념·제시 방식. 둘은 구별된다.

Frege의 Sinn(sense)/Bedeutung(reference) 구분입니다. 교재의 Superman·Clark Kent 예로 같은 지시체라도 의미(제시 방식)가 다를 수 있음을 보여주세요.

Lexical Relations

  • Synonyms — similar meaning: couch / sofa, big / large.
  • Antonyms — opposite: complementary (alive/dead), gradable (hot/cold), relational (buy/sell).
  • Homonyms / homophones — same form, unrelated senses: bat (animal / club); to / too / two.
  • Polysemy — one word, related senses: bright (light / clever).
  • Hyponyms — meaning inclusion: red is a hyponym of color; lion of feline.

※ 반의어 3종: 상보(중간 없음)·정도(중간 있음)·관계(방향 대립)로 구분.

반의어의 세 유형 구분이 시험 포인트입니다. 상보반의어는 부정하면 반대가 되지만(살아있지 않다=죽었다), 정도반의어는 그렇지 않다(뜨겁지 않다≠차갑다)는 점을 강조하세요.

Semantic Features

Word meanings can be decomposed into semantic features, much like sounds decompose into phonetic features.

Word[±human][±adult][±male]
man+++
woman++−
boy+−+
girl+−−

Features explain relations: man and boy share [+human, +male], differing only in [±adult].

※ 의미 자질 분석은 동의·반의·상하위 관계를 체계적으로 설명해 준다.

의미 자질(semantic features)은 음운 자질과 평행합니다. 자질 표기로 어휘 관계를 형식화할 수 있음을 표로 보여주세요.

5) Argument Structure & Thematic Roles

Each verb has an argument structure — how many participants it needs and what theta roles they play.

Agentthe doer — Sam opened…
Themeundergoer / receiver — …the door
Goalendpoint — …to Mary
Sourceorigin — from the shelf
Instrumentmeans — with a key
Experiencerwho perceives — Lee heard…

※ 같은 명사구라도 문장에서 맡는 의미역이 다를 수 있다 — 의미역은 통사 위치가 아니라 해석의 역할.

의미역(theta role)은 논항이 사건에서 맡는 역할입니다. 주어=행위자가 항상 성립하는 것은 아님을 강조하세요(예: The door opened에서 door는 theme).
Practice · Check Your Understanding
1"Jack boiled the water" entails "The water boiled." This relation is called:
Explanation If the first sentence is true, the second must also be true — that one-way truth relation is entailment.
함의의 단방향성을 확인하는 문항입니다. 학생이 선택 후 해설을 공개하세요.
Practice · Check Your Understanding
2"Colorless green ideas sleep furiously" is grammatical yet meaningless. This illustrates:
Explanation The sentence obeys syntactic rules but combines incompatible semantic features (colorless vs. green; ideas cannot sleep) → semantic anomaly.
통사적 적형성 ≠ 의미적 적형성. Chomsky의 고전 예로 이 구분을 확인시키세요.
Practice · Check Your Understanding
3Which pair are relational antonyms?
Explanation Relational antonyms describe the same relation from opposite viewpoints — one cannot exist without the other. hot/cold is gradable; alive/dead is complementary.
반의어 3종 구분 문항. 관계반의어는 상호 의존적 쌍(buy/sell, parent/child 등)임을 강조하세요.
Exercises · Based on Textbook Ch.4
4In "The chef sliced the bread with a knife," what theta role does a knife bear? (cf. textbook Ch.4, pp.156–158)
Explanation a knife is the means by which the action is carried out → the instrument role. The agent is the chef; the theme is the bread.

Exercises · Based on Textbook Ch.4
5Why can "The boy saw the man with the telescope" mean two different things? (cf. textbook Ch.4, pp.136–137)
Explanation This is structural (syntactic) ambiguity: with the telescope can modify saw (the boy used it) or the man (the man had it). Because meaning is compositional, different structures yield different meanings — same words.

Summary

Key Points

  • Speakers know meaning relations: entailment, paraphrase, contradiction, ambiguity
  • Meaning is compositional — words + structure
  • Compositionality fails with idioms, anomaly, metaphor
  • Word meaning: sense ≠ reference; rich lexical relations
  • Meaning decomposes into semantic features
  • Verbs assign thematic roles to their arguments
문장 의미(합성적)와 단어 의미(어휘부)라는 두 축을 정리하세요. 의미역과 논항구조는 통사론과 의미론을 잇는 다리이므로 특히 강조합니다.
