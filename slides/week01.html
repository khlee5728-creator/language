What is this course?

A deeper, systematic study of the core subfields of English linguistics — beyond an introductory survey.

The central question of this course

How do speakers produce and understand sentences they have never heard before? We uncover the underlying system of rules (grammar), layer by layer.

※ 소리→단어→문장→의미의 층위는 학기 전체의 목차이기도 합니다.

이 층위 그림은 학기 전체의 목차입니다. 이번 학기는 소리를 마지막(Ch.5–6)에 다루지만, 분석 대상은 네 층위 전부임을 짚어주세요.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand the nature and universal properties of language.
  • Analyze the morphological structure of English words.
  • Analyze syntactic structure with tree diagrams and interpret meaning.
  • Transcribe and analyze the sound system using phonetic symbols.
